# Content rules

These rules cover every theme in this gallery. They are short on purpose.

1. **Share only what is yours to share.** Every picture, video clip and sound in a theme must be something you made,
   or something you have the right to share publicly. Ticking the box in the submission form is your statement that
   this is true. It does not make it true: if it is not, the theme is removed.
2. **Nothing hateful, sexual or aimed at a person.** No harassment, no private information about anyone.
3. **A theme is a look, nothing more.** A theme is colours, a picture, an optional radar clip and optional alert sounds.
   Anything else in a submitted zip is refused automatically.
4. **Themes can be removed at any time,** with or without notice, for any of the reasons above or because the
   maintainer is not comfortable hosting them.

## What is checked, and what is not

Each submission is unpacked under strict limits, checked with the device's own theme loader, and drawn to preview
pictures. A maintainer then approves it before it appears. Those checks are about **format**. Nobody verifies where
a picture, clip or sound came from. Use themes from the gallery with that in mind.

## Report a theme

If a theme should not be here, including one that uses your work, use the **Report** link on its card in the gallery,
or open a "Report a theme" issue in this repository. Say which theme and what is wrong. Reported themes are looked at
by a person and removed when the report holds.
